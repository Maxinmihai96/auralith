Auralith – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Auralith ("the game") is a gem block puzzle game. Your privacy matters to us, and this policy explains it simply: we do not collect any personal data.

Information we collect

None. The game does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information. There are no accounts and no sign-in.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (levels, stars, scores, power-ups, chosen theme and settings) is saved only on your own device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Analytics, tracking and advertising

This version of the game contains no analytics, no tracking and no advertising. If a future update adds advertising, this policy will be updated before that version is released and will explain what the advertising provider collects.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Because we collect no data, we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
