Auralith – Support

Need help with Auralith: Gem Block Puzzle? You will find answers to common questions below.

How do I play?

Drag the crystal blocks onto the 8×8 board. Fill a whole row or column to shatter it. Clear lines back-to-back to build a combo; Combo ×3 lights up Aurora Fever with double points. The game ends when none of your blocks fit.

How do I finish a Journey level?

Free every glowing star gem by clearing the row or column it sits in. Fewer moves earn more stars.

What are ice and stone?

Ice needs extra line clears before the block breaks (thick ice needs three). Stone fills a line but never breaks; only a Bomb can smash it.

How do I get more power-ups?

Earn Undo, Bomb and Shuffle by scoring points, completing levels, opening treasure chests every 5 levels and finishing the Daily Challenge.

How do I unlock new block themes?

Collect stars in Journey mode. Tap the palette button on the home screen to choose a theme.

I lost my progress

Progress is saved on your device. Deleting the app also deletes your progress.
